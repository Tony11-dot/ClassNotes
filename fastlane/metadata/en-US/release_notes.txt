Use ClassNotes without an account, ask NOVA about a whole notebook, organise with folders and tags, and see PDFs sharp at any zoom.

- No account needed: tap Continue without an account and start writing. Sign in any time to sync and use NOVA. Your notebooks are never locked away if you're signed out.
- NOVA knows your notebook: tap Read this notebook, then ask anything about it. Answers show which pages they came from; tap a page to jump to it. When something isn't in your notes, NOVA says so.
- NOVA asks before it sends anything, explains exactly what it sends, and you can change your answer in Settings.
- Shelves inside shelves, and tags. Drag a notebook onto a shelf or tag to file it.
- PDFs you import now stay sharp however far you zoom, their words are searchable exactly, and they export with the original text.
- Lasso: rotate a selection with the new handle, and recolour it in one tap.
- Support shows crash and speed reports kept on your iPad, and you can share them with us.
- Better VoiceOver support, clearer error messages, and a fix for tape moved with the lasso landing in the wrong place.
