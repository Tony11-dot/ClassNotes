Writing you can trust.

- Everything you write stays written: page saves now land strictly in order, so a quick exit or a fast reopen can never lose your last strokes.
- What you erase stays erased, even if you double-tap the Pencil to switch tools mid-erase.
- Deleted a page by mistake? Tap Undo, or bring it back from Recently Deleted in the page manager for 30 days.
- If a save ever fails (for example, your iPad is full), ClassNotes tells you plainly, keeps your work on screen and keeps retrying.
- Your library rebuilds itself from your notebooks if its database is ever damaged, so notebooks never go missing.
- A damaged notebook file is set aside and restored from its last good copy, keeping your images, text, bookmarks and page settings.
- Notebooks deleted from the ClassMate tab now go to the Trash on your iPad instead of disappearing.
- Large notebooks open, scroll and import PDFs smoothly, and you can keep writing while a PDF imports.
- Search inside your notes is much faster, and now finds the words in PDFs, scans and photos you've imported.
- Move or copy pages to another notebook: select them in the page manager and tap Send.
- Keyboard shortcuts on iPad: Command-Z to undo, Command-1 to 5 for tools, Option-Command-Up and Down to change page, Shift-Command-N for a new page.
- The lasso holds exactly what you circled. Drag it and the content moves under your finger; circle again to reselect, tap the page to let go.
- Scribble-to-erase only removes what you actually scrubbed out, never the line beside it.
